## Portal narrative

Handshake makes agreement legible:

1. Two distinct addresses declare a negotiation.
2. Each address submits one bounded immutable position.
3. GenLayer produces a strict proposal grounded in both positions.
4. Independent validators check semantic compatibility and source grounding.
5. Both exact parties accept the persisted synthesis fingerprint.
6. The record becomes SEALED, a terminal state.

The frontend is a reader and transaction surface. The contract remains authoritative.
